Contact us

We are a small UK team and we read everything that comes in. Use the address below that matches your enquiry - it reaches the right person faster and, for data-rights requests, starts the statutory clock in the right place.

Please do not include patient-identifying information in your email. Email is not covered by the redaction pipeline that protects your recordings, and we do not need patient details to help you.

Where to write

Your enquiry Email We respond
General questions, product feedback, press, partnerships hello@logdit.app Within 14 days
Privacy, data protection, and data-subject requests - access, correction, deletion, portability, or objecting to processing privacy@logdit.app Within 1 calendar month
Suspected unauthorised access to your account, or a security concern about the Service privacy@logdit.app As soon as possible
A complaint about the Service hello@logdit.app Within 14 days

These response times are the ones we commit to in section 18 of our Terms of Service and section 11 of our Privacy Policy. If a request is complex we will tell you before the deadline rather than let it pass in silence.

App support

For help with the LOGDit app - sign-in problems, a recording that did not process, or anything that looks broken - email hello@logdit.app. It helps if you tell us your app version and device, which you can find on the Settings screen in the app.
