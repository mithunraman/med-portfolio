Questions trainees ask us

Does LOGDit replace my eportfolio?
No. LOGDit drafts the entry so you don't start from a blank box - export it as a PDF and paste the structured output into whichever eportfolio your deanery requires.
Which specialties do you support?
Today: General Practice (RCGP) only. Internal Medicine (JRCPTB), Psychiatry (RCPsych), Emergency Medicine and Paediatrics are on the roadmap. Email us with your specialty and we'll prioritise based on demand.
How does the PII redaction actually work?
Your audio is transcribed, then an automatic redaction pass replaces patient identifiers - names, NHS numbers, dates of birth, postcodes and addresses - with typed placeholders. Only this redacted text is used to draft your reflections, capability tags and PDP suggestions.
What about my recordings - where do they go?
Audio is stored encrypted in UK-region cloud infrastructure and automatically deleted within 72 hours of transcription. You can also delete any recording manually from within the app at any time, and full account deletion removes all your content.
Is it free?
Free to use today - no card required, no trial timer. We'll announce pricing before we introduce a paid plan, with advance notice and no auto-charges. Anything you've used for free stays free.
Will I still own and write my own reflections?
Yes. LOGDit drafts structured reflections from what you said, but every section is editable and version-historied. You sign off on what goes into your portfolio - same as you would today.
Who built this?
A small team of UK doctors and engineers. We can't fix everything wrong with portfolio admin, but we can fix the blank-page problem. Reach us at hello@logdit.app.
