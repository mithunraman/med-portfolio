Sub-processors

Last updated: 4 August 2026

This page lists the third-party service providers that LOGDit uses to operate the Service ("sub-processors"). It supplements section 8 of our Privacy Policy. Material changes - such as adding a sub-processor, removing one, or changing the role of an existing one - are published here; see How to be notified of changes below.

Each sub-processor below is bound by a written data processing agreement that restricts use of your personal data to the purposes described.

No sub-processor uses your content to train AI or machine learning models. For most of them this is a prohibition written into their data processing agreement. The exception is our transcription provider, AssemblyAI, whose standard terms permit model training by default: we have opted out of their model improvement programme, and we did so before the Service carried any real user content.

* A United States company. Your data is stored in the location shown, but the provider may access it from outside the UK and EEA for support, incident response and recovery.

Every transfer outside the UK and EEA is covered by a recognised legal safeguard - depending on the provider, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum, Binding Corporate Rules, or the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework - together with our own supplementary measures, which are described in section 9 of our Privacy Policy. A copy of the safeguards applying to any particular provider is available on request at privacy@logdit.app.

How to be notified of changes

The simplest way to be notified is to bookmark this page and re-check the "Last updated" date above, or to subscribe to product update emails when you create your account. Where we add or replace a sub-processor, we will update this page before the change takes effect and give as much notice as our own agreements with these providers allow. Where practicable we will also notify active users by email or in-app. Changes prompted by a security or operational incident will be published as soon as practicable.

Questions about our sub-processors, or a request to receive proactive email notification of changes: privacy@logdit.app.
